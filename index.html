A little about us

From a distance, we are all silhouettes against the light.

Defined by an outline, a shape, a presence. It is only when we look closer that we begin to notice what makes each of us different. Beyond the first impression lies a story told through proportions, textures, materials, light and the details that might otherwise go unnoticed. These are the elements that give a space its character and make it distinct.

Silhouette is led by Mannat Khurana — architecturally trained, with a Master of Architecture (Professional) from the University of Auckland — a designer, a writer and an artist whose pieces are made for the rooms they hang in. The studio is built on the idea that what makes something exceptional is not always immediately visible. It is found in looking closer, understanding what exists and recognising the potential in what could be.

the difference

When an interior is designed by someone trained in architecture, it's different: the value and understanding of architecture and engineering are merged into how the space looks, feels and works.

  • Structure and services, understood

    Layouts that work with the building, not against it — walls, plumbing, ventilation and structure considered from the first sketch.

  • Drawings that build

    Interiors documented with architectural precision, so builders, joiners and engineers can work from them without guesswork.

  • Better decisions, earlier

    Knowing what's possible — and what a change will involve — means the big decisions are made early, when they cost least.

  • The right specialists, one contact

    From engineers to fabricators, Mannat sources and coordinates the specialists each project needs — or works alongside your own architect and team.

Questions, answered

good to know
What does an interior designer do?

An interior designer shapes how a space works and how it feels — planning the layout; choosing materials, colours and finishes; designing joinery and lighting; selecting or designing furniture and art. At Silhouette we also source everything and see it installed, so the finished space matches the design.

Why choose an architecturally trained interior designer?

Because an interior is never designed in isolation. Mannat's architectural training brings an understanding of structure, services and engineering to every interior — so layouts work with the building, drawings are precise enough to build from, and fewer surprises appear on site. It's the difference between decorating a space and designing it.

Can you work with my architect?

Yes. Mannat works alongside your architect and engineer to develop the interiors in the same language — or, if you're starting fresh, brings in the right specialists, from engineers to fabricators, when your project needs them.

Do you only work in Auckland?

No. The studio is based in Auckland and designs homes and commercial spaces across New Zealand and Australia — including Sydney, Melbourne and Perth — and take on international projects by arrangement. Implementation is scoped to suit each project. Where we work.

Do you design commercial spaces as well as homes?

Yes. Recent commercial work includes an orthodontic clinic fit-out and a retail store for OGT, alongside residential interiors and renovations.

Can you design bespoke furniture, lighting and art?

Yes. We design furniture, joinery and lighting for the spaces they'll live in, source artefacts from New Zealand and overseas, and Mannat creates one-off statement artworks by hand. Bespoke furniture, lighting & art.

Do you handle sourcing and installation?

Yes — specification, procurement, trades coordination, installation and final styling. Designed by Mannat, delivered by the studio. Sourcing & implementation.

Who builds and makes the design?

The studio sources and coordinates the specialists each project needs — engineers, registered architects where consent calls for them, builders, joiners, fabricators and installers — or works with yours. One point of contact, from the first sketch to the final styling.

How much does an interior designer cost?

Fees depend on scope — a consultation, a whole home or a commercial fit-out are each priced differently. After a first conversation about your space, you'll receive a clear fee proposal before any design work begins.

Will I see the design before it's built?

Yes. Spaces are modelled in 3D and rendered, so you can see the layout, materials and lighting together before anything is built — from wherever you are.

How does a project start?

With a conversation. Send a few lines about your space — location, scope and timing — and we'll be in touch to understand your brief. That's the Discover stage, before Design, Develop and Deliver.
