Where we work · Australia

Interior design for Melbourne

Period homes, townhouses and a city that takes design seriously — interiors with texture, warmth and quiet confidence.

Melbourne is one of our primary markets — a city with a deep design culture and a climate that rewards warmth and texture. The studio designs Melbourne homes and commercial spaces from Auckland, around two hours ahead, with site visits planned at the moments that matter.

Period character, modern living

Victorian and Edwardian terraces and weatherboards bring proportion, ornament and history; modern life asks for open kitchens, light and storage. We design interiors that respect the original character while making it work for how you live now.

Warmth for four seasons in a day

Melbourne winters ask more of an interior. We lean into tactile materials — timber, wool, plaster, stone — and layered, dimmable light, so rooms feel generous on grey days and open in summer.

Local makers, international reach

We work with Melbourne makers and suppliers alongside the studio's New Zealand and overseas relationships, and coordinate with your local builder and trades through to final styling.

Questions, answered

melbourne
Can you design for a heritage home in Melbourne?

Yes — we design interiors that respect a home's period character. Where a heritage overlay or permit applies, we work alongside your local architect, builder or planner.

How do we meet if you're based in Auckland?

Over video calls and shared 3D visuals for the design, with planned site visits at key stages.
